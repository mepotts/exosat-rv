# M37 — Audit correction: the 171-day result is conditional, not independent

A parallel, adversarial audit was run before starting new science. It checked the central
RV claim from the retained tables, the extraction-selection history, injection semantics,
M35 photometry, M36's implementation, nested-sampling summaries, transfer targets, release
metadata, and whether a clone could reproduce the load-bearing calculations.

**Bottom line.** The downstream period signal remains strong after the project's internal
quality screen, but the complete archival series is not a detection. The excluded night is
therefore load-bearing. The extraction was calibrated with the published RV series visible,
and the injections begin after template construction, so “independent end-to-end
reproduction” and “end-to-end injection” were both too strong. Those phrases are withdrawn.

---

## 1. What this milestone supersedes

This record does not erase the earlier milestones; it narrows their conclusions.

- **M14/M28:** the leave-one-out result establishes that no *retained* night carries the
  period. It says nothing about robustness to restoring the internally rejected night.
- **M15:** eta Tel B remains a useful same-setting nodding control and its sensitivity curve
  remains numerically reproducible. The curve is a grid-pointwise, circular-orbit,
  90%-completeness sensitivity conditional on fitter-stage transmission—not an unconditional
  confidence upper limit.
- **M17/M20–M23:** transfer across both observing modes is not established. The supposed
  staring observations were HiRISE fibre data processed with a slit recipe; the beta Pic
  extraction is host-dominated and is not a companion RV measurement.
- **M35:** the photometric null survives, but its original exposure-level permutation and
  sparse phase experiment overstated sensitivity. Gaia RUWE/NSS are catalogue diagnostics,
  not proof that no astrometric perturbation exists.
- **M36:** the recorded run did not faithfully execute its preregistration. Its conservative
  “inconclusive” interpretation remains safe, but `data/m36-selection.json` is not a valid
  preregistered result.

The RV table answers two distinct questions. A fixed, pre-specified window near the reported
period can still look unusual in some complete-series variants, but after accounting for the
largest peak searched over the grid—and especially after including BERV—the complete series
is compatible with noise under this calibration. On the screened series, the near-171-day
peak is also the global peak and has nominal `p_global < 0.01` for all three combination rules.
That probability assumes exchangeable fitted residuals and does not pay for choosing the
screen, so it is not confirmatory significance. The defensible descriptive claim is explicitly
conditional on the screen.

The corrected photometry similarly supports a null, but a weaker one: no coherent host-star
modulation is detected at the RV period, with the sensitivity reported above. This does not
exclude line-profile activity with weak broadband modulation, and the companion itself is
too faint for ASAS-SN. The fixed-period permutation probabilities are nominal and conditional
on exchangeability of the final camera-corrected night bins; camera/season heteroskedasticity
has not been calibrated. The K90 phase fractions are likewise conditional on the one observed
residual/noise realisation, the preprocessing, and the estimated fixed-period permutation
threshold. They are deterministic phase-grid sensitivity summaries, not repeated-noise
coverage statements.

## 3. Why M36 is invalid as a preregistered execution

The protocol fixed `chunks=1`, `deg_norm=3`, `deg_wave=3`, and `iset=380:1700`. The historical
runner passed none of those options explicitly; the audited `[CRIRES]` configuration uses
different polynomial degrees. In addition, `inject_score2.py` formed injected and reference
means from independently valid order sets, and the runner parsed recovery values after
rounding them to integer percentages. Existing result files were accepted by filename alone,
without binding them to inputs or configuration.

There is a separate independence failure. `inject_plan_big.json` encodes a signal evaluated
on the published 171.454-day orbit. The historical search imports the published epoch table
to define a matched subset and hard-codes a window around 171.45 days. Published *velocity*
values do not enter its least-squares fit, but neither the selector nor the search is genuinely
paper-blind.

The implementation is now hardened:

- injected/reference differences use the same intersection of valid orders;
- machine-readable scores retain full precision;
- every registered fixed option is explicit; and
- reusable RV/template products require content- and configuration-bound manifests.

Those fixes are prospective. The runner preserves M36's registered slope-only gate for
inspection, but non-dry execution is disabled before external work or artifact creation. Its
dormant paths use an `M36PA_` namespace and run-specific artifact names; they cannot overwrite
or rehabilitate the historical artifact. No replay was run in this milestone. A different
uncertainty-aware gate or template rule belongs to a new protocol.

| “blind period search” | published RV values do not enter the regression, but the workflow imports published epochs, hard-codes the published-period window, uses a paper-derived injection plan, and follows extraction/order/template choices made with the paper visible |
| “global FAP” without stating the data subset | the calibrated global probability must always say complete or screened and whether BERV is included |
| “end-to-end injection” | wavelength-shifted, already-built templates test fitter-stage transmission, not absorption during template construction |
| “eta Tel upper limit” | pointwise circular-orbit completeness sensitivity, conditional on the adopted extraction and fitter-stage transmission |
| “second satellite contradicted generally” | not reproduced under this project's stated models and priors; a different sampler was not tested |
| “transfers across both observing modes” | strongest clean control is eta Tel B in the same-setting nodding mode; HiRISE/slit claims are withdrawn |
| “clean Gaia astrometry” | ordinary RUWE/no NSS are context only; they cannot exclude a perturbation |

The nested-sampling audit also corrected bookkeeping in the manuscript: table rows now carry
their actual replicate counts, “runs” are distinguished from paired comparisons, and no claim
is extrapolated to samplers that were not executed here.

## 5. What is reproducible now—and what is not

`data/repro/` freezes the adopted CD-35 2722 B and eta Tel B RV tables, per-order parameter
tables, target sidecars, audited VIPER configuration, and the tracked VIPER patch. The
manifest hashes the fitted templates and FTS atlas that remain external. Downstream exports,
the all-versus-screened audit, jackknife, and eta Tel sensitivity calibration now default to
the bundled tables.

This closes the former “small final products live only in `~/viper-src`” gap. It does **not**
make the project raw-to-RV reproducible: ESO exposures, reduced spectra, and fitted templates
are not bundled; historical environment and effective-setting snapshots were not captured at
run time; and the audit cannot prove when the current external patch/configuration was applied.

## 6. Current scientific verdict

What survives is narrower but still useful:

1. On the internally screened 17-night CD-35 series, a near-171-day peak is the strongest
   searched period and survives a BERV covariate under all three combination rules, with
   nominal conditional permutation probabilities below 0.01.
2. On all 18 nights, no combination produces nominal BERV-adjusted `p_global < 0.05`.
3. The screened-series leave-one-out test shows the result is not carried by any one of the
   retained nights; this is a conditional robustness check, not a cure for point 2.
4. Host photometry has no detected coherent modulation at the RV period at the corrected
   sensitivity. Gaia adds context, not an exclusion.
5. eta Tel B remains the clean transfer/null control, with the injection and limit scopes
   stated narrowly.
6. The smaller companion is not supported by this project's tested models and priors.

Accordingly, none of the current manuscripts is submission-ready on the strength of an
“independent reproduction” claim. No submission, release, tag, push, or external action was
taken.

## 8. Next milestone boundary

The successor to M36 needs a new protocol with a paper-blind template-convergence rule,
common-order injection scores, uncertainty-aware eligibility, immutable caches, and a held-out
period search. The existing 18 epochs are development data; genuine observer blindness needs
an uncontaminated executor or prospective untouched epochs. `M38-PROTOCOL-DRAFT.md` records
the unresolved design without authorizing a run. It is **not preregistered** until reviewed
and committed before execution. No such experiment was started here.
